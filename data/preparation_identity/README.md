# Preparation identity and exposure interpretation

This six-record curation distinguishes the preparations discussed in Route B2 and the conclusions each supports. It accompanies article v37. The table is a reporting aid; it is not an executable gate, a new biological dataset or a source of clinical dose conversions.

## Current interpretation

- **JGW03:** the deposited cell-line transcriptomic comparisons remain interpretable for the study preparation. Chemistry calibration/unit and summary-label issues and the unresolved chemistry-assay-to-RNA-seq batch link prevent accepted absolute constituent-exposure conversions. Preparation identity alone does not establish quantitatively validated batch chemistry.
- **GSE205906 PLM:** the three submitted PLM labels denote one nominal treatment group. The completed group comparison does not depend on an author reply. Conflicting treatment strings remain unresolved for actual prepared doses, and animal/library correspondence, pooling and complete batch structure remain unverified. PLM1-3 must not be treated as a continuous dose series. These are spinal samples after spared peripheral nerve injury; the preparation is a two-compound combination. It supplies exploratory context, not whole-formula or PSS efficacy evidence and not a completed sequential candidate validation after the zero S1 result.
- **TJ-68:** human plasma measurements support exposure context for the stated product, lot and analytes. They do not establish unbound spinal concentrations or an exposure-equivalent JGW03 dose. Total extract mass and individual circulating constituents have different measurement bases.
- **Other entries:** JGT03 in mouse muscle atrophy, a Chinese analytical exemplar and a clinically studied glucoside fraction retain their distinct source-specific roles. No cross-product equivalence is assumed.

The historical project identity table is unchanged. Its older JGW03 chemistry wording and GSE205906 author-clarification status are qualified by this dated reporting view. No source string, scientific input, model result, group assignment or candidate rule was changed.

## Reading the table

Each row identifies the material class, context, supported interpretation, exposure limit and unresolved source issue. The source URLs identify original records; some web hosts were unavailable during the final update, and retained prior inspections underpin those entries. A source-level uncertainty is not a claim that all results from that study are invalid.

`PROVENANCE.json` gives the curation date, scope and hashes of retained supporting records. This public extension contains original explanatory curation and source identifiers, not article PDFs, chemical workbooks, individual records or private review documents.
