# Source attribution and scope

Source: National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI), National Library of Medicine, NCBI Datasets gene reports, taxa 9606/10090/10116. Fields are GeneID, gene symbol, gene-group identifier/method, taxonomic ID/name and gene type. This supplement contains factual molecular mapping records, not articles, abstracts, clinical records or participant genotypes.

NCBI molecular-data usage policy states that NCBI itself imposes no use/distribution restrictions, while noting possible third-party intellectual-property claims and that NCBI cannot transfer those rights. The snapshot is retained for exact computational reproducibility. Fresh API responses can differ and must be separately versioned. Original local records remain the evidence source; this candidate is not an official NCBI archival release.
